## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: authored for this product. The six-place stage track that marks a position and is never filled, amber meaning only "promised", headlines as attributed quotations, and the then/now cell are specific. But the visual vocabulary stops at the status digest. Timetable, trials, papers, organisations and background are prose in one grey-and-ink rhythm. The knee osteoarthritis claim is 33 phone screens.

Deterministic scan: source scan of `site/src` returned 0 findings. Browser scan on five pages returned about 100 runtime findings: about 52 `line-length` (38 on `/how-it-works`, prose at 91 to 99 characters; the footer "Data sources" paragraph at about 89 characters counts once per page), 7 `straggler`, 1 `skipped-heading` on home (h2 then h4), 1 `cramped-padding` on a claim page's `div.brief-group` (possible false positive). The detector agrees with the review on one point: the site's problem is volume and measure of text, not decoration.

Visual overlays: injected and scanned in the detector's own tab, which was then closed. No overlay is left visible.

## Overall Impression

A strong, narrow visual language (track, amber, grounds) is wrapped in sentences that repeat what the marks already say, and everything below the digest has no marks at all. Biggest opportunity: one "record strip" (records on a time axis) reused on rows, tables, cards and the claim timetable.

## What's Working

- Amber plus dashed rule means promised and nothing else, on every page seen.
- The stage track marks a place and is never filled, so it cannot read as progress owed.
- StatusMade and the about page's claim-at-scale explain the product as a picture.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Claim timetable is prose.** 79 events on the knee claim, 8,341px on a phone; "Trial registered" 33 times, "Trial started" 29 times, "View trial details here" on every line (`pages/[slug].astro:506-672`, `TrialDetailsLink.astro:7`). Fix: one lane per trial on a shared year axis: hollow ring at registration, bar from start to completion (dashed while under way), square at results posted, amber dashed flag for a promised year, trial ID once as the link. Shows duration, overlap and the gap from report to first trial, which the text does not. Command: /impeccable shape, then /impeccable layout.
- **[P1] One home report row fills one phone screen.** "Evidence when first reported" and "On record now" print 18 times each; home is 15,164px (`DayReport.astro:56-68`, `lib/row-sentences.ts`, `StatusCell.astro:22`). Fix: one track with a hollow ring for the place when reported and the solid dot for now; a 120 to 160px record strip replaces "No record is dated in the 9 years since the first report" style sentences, which become its accessible name. Axis stays open-ended to the last check so an empty stretch reads as time elapsed, not an ending. Command: /impeccable distill.
- **[P1] Vocabulary ends at the digest.** Trial facts ("Assigned at random", "Blinded", "Under way"), enrolment, confidence ("High match · 99%" 12 times on one page), provenance lines (same "record read" date 8 times) and organisations (same unmatched sentence 12 times) are words only (`Trial.astro:73-115`, `Paper.astro:67`, `Organisations.astro`). Fix: group by match band with the score in a tabular column; state read date once per section; organisations as a compact table with KindIcon role column; fixed-order glyph strip for trial design with no empty-means-absent slot. Command: /impeccable distill, /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] Four DESIGN.md rules cap this lens and need an operator decision.** "Words carry status" (line 11), status gloss wherever a status shows (line 102), "every trial is drawn at one size" (line 91), "every record line says its kind in words" (line 220). Proposal: keep status label in sight always; move gloss, kind and repeated captions to a key plus accessible names. Command: /impeccable document after the decision.
- **[P2] Status drawn inconsistently; off-path statuses have no glyph.** Track sits left of the label in tables, under it in cells, right of it in `DayRecord.astro:61`. "No trial record found" and "Origin paper retracted" are words only (`StageTrack.astro:17`). Fix: one placement; off-path as six hollow places with a ring set off the line. No cross, strike or grey-out. Command: /impeccable polish.

## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: its own thing. Navy band, amber-means-promised, stage track and strip marks are a real vocabulary. Weakness: each new drawing is added beside the words it could replace, and the site now runs three mark vocabularies that contradict each other in places. The strip is the right idea, drawn on a scale that hides most of it on today's data.

Deterministic scan: source scan 0 findings. Browser scan on five pages: 54 findings, 43 `line-length`, 9 `straggler`, 1 `skipped-heading` (home h2 to h4), 1 `edge-flush-cards` on the section nav scroller (`ul.strip`, likely intended). None on the record strip. The scan's `ul.strip` finding exposed a class-name collision with the strip's SVG, fixed in 574edc7.

## Overall Impression

The strip works where records exist and reads as an empty line where they do not, which is most of the site today. The larger problem is restatement: track dot, label, gloss, "Tested in people", strip and counts say overlapping things on one row.

The record in brief What the reports said 3 Promised and recorded 21 Trials on record 4 Organisations named 5

A randomised, placebo-controlled trial of solengepras in 341 patients in the US, Europe, the UK and Australia found that the higher dose reduced daily ‘off’ time by about 37 minutes more than placebo. The company, Cerevance, will take the results to the FDA to decide next steps.

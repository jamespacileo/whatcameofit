Left out, and what needs your call

Not borrowed

  • Grades and coloured outcome rows.The A to D grades are Examine’s core device and the one thing the product forbids. The card shows a status in words and its place on the path, nothing else.
  • Serif headings.Examine sets titles in Lora. The mocks stay in Figtree; one typeface is a principle in DESIGN.md.
  • Author portraits and the “Evidence-based” badge.No person is named on our pages, and a badge would be a claim about ourselves.
  • Locked sections and sign-up boxes inside the reading column.

Changes to rules in DESIGN.md

  • Navy band under the masthead.Chosen. Today a solid navy block is allowed only for the masthead and footer, so the rule gains the band.
  • Timeline order.Newest first in the mock; the timetable is oldest first today.
  • Promised beside recorded.Kept. A year with a promise carries two boxes on the line: what was promised for it, on amber, beside what was recorded in it. See the second claim mock.
  • Page width.78rem with side columns, against 60rem today.
  • Trial entries.A table row per trial replaces the stacked entry DESIGN.md describes; the plain wording is kept.

Tourette syndrome: The life-changing device helping to stop tics  BBC News · bbc.co.uk · 12 Nov 2026

A BBC News video report described a small wrist device developed at the University of Nottingham that delivers electrical pulses to the median nerve, which trials have shown can change activity in brain regions involved in movement control. It features a 15-year-old user who said tics stopped quickly. The piece does not give trial sizes or results, so the evidence is described only in general terms.

Status card: hover and focus popovers

Four ways to say what “On record now” and the six dots mean, drawn on the Tourette row from the home page. Each uses the site’s existing popover (white, hairline, the one soft shadow, 170 ms rise) except D, which opens in place.

What this row shows: the card says Trial registered, but its strip says 3 trials with results posted. All three trials started and completed; ClinicalTrials.gov records each one’s phase as “not applicable”, as it usually does for device studies, so by the rule none counts as a phase. A reader sees both on the same row and has nothing to reconcile them with. B and D answer that for this claim.

A. The path, named

Smallest change

Hover or focus the status name (dotted underline, the site’s existing “more” trigger). The six dots become six named places, one line of plain words under each place that is not obvious. The same for every claim at this status.

D. The card opens in place

No floating layer

On hover or focus the card widens over its own column: the dots get names, then the gloss and the reason for this claim. Nothing floats, so nothing covers the headline, and on a touch screen it can simply be shown open, as the row’s “when first reported” line already is.

The mark, moving, and drawings that move once

The mark is a glass held over a capsule: someone looking at what was promised. These studies let it move the way the rest of the site already moves. A line is drawn once, a thing arrives and lands, and then it is still.

Held throughout: nothing loops unless a reader is waiting, nothing moves on scroll, every study rests on the finished drawing, and with reduced motion the finished drawing is all there is.

The mark

The masthead already has one authored moment: the question is drawn and its dot lands. A study for the masthead has to join that sentence, not start a second one. Each study plays with the name beside it, at the masthead's size, and on white.

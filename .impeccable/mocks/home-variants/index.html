                          About this site

                          Held in all three: an outlet’s headline is a quotation with the outlet named, never the site’s own words; a status is the site’s label with its place on the path, not a verdict; “no signal” is said as the site says it; no numbers are shown that the export does not hold. The records drawn are published claims, read from the export this mock was built from.

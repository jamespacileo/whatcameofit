The FDA rejected Summit Therapeutics’ application for ivonescimab, saying the global trial did not show a statistically significant survival benefit. The article says the trial found the drug delayed cancer progression when given with chemotherapy. Summit attributed the survival result to slow enrollment and submitted additional analyses, which did not persuade regulators.

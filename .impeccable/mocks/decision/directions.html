Three calmer directions for the claim page

Same page, same content, same structure as now: status first, then what was promised beside what was recorded. Only the visual world changes. Each sketch shows the top of the real seeded claim.

None of them grades or rates anything: the product's rule against verdicts holds in all three.

A · Clinical record

The look of a public health register: a deep blue header, one plain sans, a bordered record strip. Familiar from ClinicalTrials.gov and national health sites. Most sober of the three. Risk: can read as official guidance, which this site is not.

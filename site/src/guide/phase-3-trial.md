## In the records

A trial registry holds a record of a clinical trial of the treatment for the condition, matched to the claim. The record gives the trial's phase as Phase 3, and the registry shows the trial as started.

“Furthest” means the highest phase among the claim's trials that have started. A claim can have several trials at different phases; the status names one of them, and the claim page lists them all.

A Phase 3 trial is a large, late-stage trial in people. The registry record says how many people the trial plans to enrol and what it sets out to measure; the claim page links that record.

## What it does not tell you

It is not a result. A large late-stage trial in people has started; what it finds is not known until results are reported, and the status does not change when they are.

It is not an approval. A regulator decides on approval separately, after a trial like this, and this site records that decision as its own status.

It does not say the trial has finished recruiting, or that it will run to its end.

## In the records

The first news reports of the claim described a study in people, and that is recorded with the claim. A search of the trial registries this site checks then found no clinical trial of the treatment for the condition.

So the page holds reports of a study in people and no registry record to set beside them. The status says exactly that, and no more.

## What it does not tell you

It does not say that no trial took place. A study in people can be missing from a search for several reasons: it was registered under a name the search did not match, it sits in a registry this site does not check, it was a kind of study that is not always registered, or it was never registered.

It does not say the reports were wrong, and it does not say the study was small or large. Those are not things a missing record can show.

It does not say whether the treatment works. When a matching trial record is found, the status changes to the one that record supports.

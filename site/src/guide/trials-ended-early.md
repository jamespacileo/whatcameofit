## In the records

Each trial matched to the claim has a registry record, and each of those records now gives the trial's state as terminated or withdrawn. The claim page lists the trials with the state the registry gives and the date it changed.

A registry record can carry the sponsor's own short reason for stopping. Where it does, the claim page shows it as the registry wrote it.

## What it does not tell you

It does not say why, unless the registry does. The reason a registry shows is a short note in the sponsor's own words, and there is no fixed list of reasons a trial may stop.

It does not say the treatment was found to be unsafe, or found not to work. The status describes the trials, not the treatment.

It does not say research on the treatment has ended. Work elsewhere, or for another condition, is outside this claim.

A newly registered trial that counts would change the status.

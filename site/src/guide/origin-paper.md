## In the records

Many reported breakthroughs begin with one published paper: a journal article describing a laboratory result, an animal study or a study in people. Where the first reports of a claim were about such a paper, this site records which paper it was, by its identifier in the publishing record.

Two things are then looked for against it: later papers that cite it, and any retraction notice issued for it.

Not every claim has an origin paper. Some first reports are about a conference talk, a company announcement or a trial starting, and then none is recorded.

## What it does not tell you

Naming a paper as the origin does not say the paper was sound, and does not say the news reports described it accurately. This site does not review papers.

It does not say the paper was the first work on the treatment. It is only the one the first reports were about.

A claim with no origin paper is not weaker for it. The field is simply empty, and the claim's status is set from its other records.

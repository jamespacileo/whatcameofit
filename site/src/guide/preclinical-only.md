## In the records

Two things stand behind this status. The first is an absence: a search of the trial registries this site checks found no clinical trial of the treatment for the condition. The second is the kind of study the first news reports described, which is recorded with the claim as laboratory work (cells or tissue) or an animal study.

A claim page with this status may still list other records, such as a follow-up paper. None of them is a trial registry record.

## What it does not tell you

It does not say the research stopped. Work that has not been published or registered leaves no record to find.

It does not say the treatment has never been given to a person. A trial can be registered under a name the search did not match, in a registry this site does not check, or not registered at all.

It does not say whether the treatment works, in animals or in people. The status describes which records exist, and nothing about what they found.

A new trial record moves the claim to another status the next time the registries are checked.

## In the records

A record is one entry on a claim page's timeline. Each has a kind, a date and a source: a trial registry record (a trial registered, started, completed, terminated, or its results posted), a paper, a regulator's decision, a retraction notice, a grant or a company event.

The fact comes from a registry or database, and the link leads to that source's own page. The short summary beside it is written by this site. Where the source gives only a month or a year, the date says so.

## What it does not tell you

A record says that something was entered in a public source on a date. It does not say what the event means for the treatment, and this site adds no reading of it.

The absence of a record is not a record. Work that was never registered or published cannot be found, so a quiet page shows only that nothing was found in the sources checked.

A record is only as current as the source. A registry entry can be out of date, and the claim page gives the day the registries were last checked.

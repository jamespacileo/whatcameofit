## In the records

A result has been reported for one of the claim's trials in which the primary endpoint, the main thing the trial set out to measure, was not met. A person has read that report and marked the trial, and the claim page links the source.

No other trial of the treatment for the condition is left standing: none that is neither terminated nor withdrawn, and unmarked.

## What it does not tell you

It describes one trial's main planned measure. It does not say that every measure in the trial went the same way, or what the trial found on safety.

It does not say the treatment could not meet a different endpoint, at a different dose, or in a different group of people. Those would be other trials, and none is on record.

It is not this site's judgement of the treatment, the researchers or the company. The wording is the reported result, and the link shows where it was reported.

A newly registered trial that counts would change the status.

## In the records

A trial registry holds a record of a clinical trial of the treatment for the condition, and that record has been matched to the claim. Either the registry does not yet show the trial as started, or the record gives no phase.

The claim page links the record, so the registry's own account of the trial is one press away: who sponsors it, what it plans to measure and its current state.

## What it does not tell you

A registration is a plan made public. It does not say that anyone has been enrolled, that the trial will start on the date given, or that it will run to its end.

It does not say the treatment is close to use. Most of the path lies after this place.

It does not say the treatment works or is safe. A trial is registered in order to find that out.

It is not a list of places to receive the treatment. A registered clinical trial is not a treatment option.

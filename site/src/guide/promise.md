## In the records

A promise comes from a news report, not from a registry. Where a report states when something is expected, such as a trial starting or a treatment reaching patients, this site notes the statement in a few words, the date it names, and the report it came from.

A claim can have several promises, from different reports. Lists on this site show the latest one that names a date. On a claim page the promises sit on the same timeline as the records, so what was said and what was recorded can be read against each other.

## What it does not tell you

It does not say who made the promise. A timeline in a news report may come from a researcher, a company, a press release or the journalist's own summary, and the report is the place to see which.

It does not say the promise was broken when its date has passed. Records can lag behind events, and work that is not registered or published leaves nothing to find. This site shows the promised date and the records, and draws no conclusion from the gap.

It does not say the promise was reasonable or unreasonable when it was made.

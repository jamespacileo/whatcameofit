## In the records

Registries do not know which news reports a trial belongs to. This site searches them for the treatment's names and the condition, and scores each record it finds. The score is shown as a word and a percentage, such as “High match · 92%”, with the reasons for it.

A match is either approved, in which case its record counts toward the claim's status, or a candidate, which is listed and does not count. A trial matches a claim only when it tests the treatment for the claim's condition.

The same wording is used when a reader asks for a live registry search of a report this site does not track. Those answers are marked automatic and unreviewed.

## What it does not tell you

Confidence is about the match, never about the treatment. A high score does not say a treatment works, and a low one does not say it does not.

It is not a measured probability. It is an estimate made by software, and it can be wrong in both directions: a record can be matched that belongs to another treatment, and a real trial can be missed.

It is not a rating of a trial's quality or size.

## In the records

The first news reports of the claim were about a published paper, which this site calls the origin paper. The journal that published it has since issued a retraction notice, and the claim page lists that notice as a record, with its date and a link.

Any trials and other records found for the claim stay on its page. They no longer set the status.

## What it does not tell you

It does not say why the paper was retracted. Papers are retracted for honest error as well as for misconduct, and the notice itself is where the journal gives its reason.

It does not say that everything in the paper was wrong, or that later work by other groups is affected. A retraction is about one paper.

It does not say the treatment does not work. It says the paper that the first reports relied on is no longer part of the published record.

It is not this site's judgement of the authors or the journal.

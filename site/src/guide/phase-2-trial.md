## In the records

A trial registry holds a record of a clinical trial of the treatment for the condition, matched to the claim. The record gives the trial's phase as Phase 2, and the registry shows the trial as started.

“Furthest” means the highest phase among the claim's trials that have started. A claim can have several trials at different phases; the status names one of them, and the claim page lists them all.

A Phase 2 trial comes after the earliest trials in people. The registry record says how many people the trial plans to enrol and what it sets out to measure; the claim page links that record.

## What it does not tell you

It does not say the treatment works. A trial at this stage is still gathering early evidence on effect and safety, in a limited number of people.

It does not say the trial has finished, or that it has reported anything. “Started” is a state in the registry, not a result.

It does not say that earlier phases were completed for this same use, or that a Phase 3 trial will follow.

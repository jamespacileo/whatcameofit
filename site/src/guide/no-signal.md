## In the records

The note rests on dates alone. This site takes the most recent record attached to the claim, or the date of the first news report where there is none, and counts the whole years since. Past a fixed number of years the claim page shows the count, such as “No signal for 5 years”.

It is added only to statuses that further evidence could still move: “Preclinical only” through “Phase 3 trial”, and “No trial record found”. It is not a status, and the status under it is unchanged.

## What it does not tell you

It does not say the work has stopped. Research that has not been published or registered leaves no record, and a long gap between records is ordinary in medical research.

It does not say why nothing new is on record. Funding, results, a change of plans and plain delay all look the same from here.

It is not a verdict on the treatment or on the people working on it. “No record since 2021” is the whole of what is known.

It does not say nothing will be recorded next month. The registries are checked again, and the note goes when a record arrives.

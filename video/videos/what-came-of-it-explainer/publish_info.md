## Title

What came of it? What happened after the medical breakthrough headline

Shorter, for social posts: What happened after the headline?

## Description

News reports medical breakthroughs. The follow-up is rarely reported.

“What came of it?” keeps a page for each reported breakthrough: one treatment, for one condition. Each page shows where the claim stands now and the dated, sourced records that say so. The status is set by fixed rules from public registry records. No editor and no AI model decides it.

Paste an article's link at https://whatcameofit.com to see the tracked claim's status, or use the browser extension for Chrome and Safari on the article itself.

This site gives no medical advice and recommends no treatment. A registered clinical trial is not a treatment option. Talk to a clinician about your own care.

Data: ClinicalTrials.gov, Europe PMC, Crossref. Records shown were checked in November 2026.

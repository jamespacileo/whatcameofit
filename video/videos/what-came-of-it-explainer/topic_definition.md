# Topic definition

- **Subject:** what “What came of it?” is, shown through one claim page, the link lookup and the browser extension.
- **Where it runs:** the site's docs, social posts, YouTube. One script, two shapes (16:9 and 9:16).
- **Viewer:** someone who remembers a breakthrough headline and wonders what became of it. No trial vocabulary assumed.
- **Length:** under two minutes.
- **Voice:** the site's own: plain, exact, unexcited. British English narrator. No call to like or subscribe; the close is the name and the address.
- **Look:** the site's visual system (`DESIGN.md`), not a video template: white ground, one face, one accent, pale amber for a promise only, no colour for a status.

# Design

Operate mode; refine the existing Figtree/navy/white identity. Use a compact automatic/unreviewed disclosure, treatment heading and indication, then one card per candidate. Titles remain complete and link to the registry. Match confidence is a blue labelled figure, not a treatment rating or traffic-light grade. Phase and recruitment state are neutral text with supporting icons. Native details/summary provide keyboard-accessible disclosure for each record and for sources/privacy. A short medical limitation stays visible. Search again, screenshot preview and settings retain their actions in a quiet row. No network or permission changes.

The shared site wording adds structured subject and registry fields while retaining its current prose interface for the website and older cached answers. The extension tolerates missing new fields. Long titles wrap; information never depends on colour or hover. First-use consent stays explicit and fully expanded.

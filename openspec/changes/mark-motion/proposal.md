# Let the mark move, and four drawings move once

## Why

The mark (a glass over a capsule) was a still image everywhere, and the site's few
moving parts were the question mark of the name and the stage marker. On 2026-11-14
the operator asked for ideas for subtle motion in the mark and for drawn assets that
move, looked at ten live studies (`.impeccable/mocks/motion/directions.html`), and
asked for the suggested set and the four drawings to be built.

## What changes

- **Masthead.** The mark is drawn into the page. On the first page of a visit the
  glass is lowered onto the capsule, then the question is drawn and its dot lands:
  one sentence, 1.6 s. Hovering the name no longer plays the question again; the
  capsule is seen closer and settles.
- **Nothing under the glass.** The not-found page, and a search with no result, show
  the path with no place marked and a glass that passes over it and comes to rest.
- **How a status is made.** The method page gains a drawing under the video: reported,
  recorded, where it stands. Its example is made up and says so. It plays in order
  only when the reader asks.
- **Topic chips.** A topic's drawing is drawn again on hover or focus.
- **Confirmations.** Follow's bookmark lands on the press. A saved reminder turns its
  clock's hand once and says "Saved to your calendar" in the pill pressed.
- **Extension.** While the popup waits, the capsule passes beneath the glass, in
  place of the breathing dot. The tick of "You follow this claim." is drawn on the
  press.
- **Video.** A four-second sting of the mark and the name, in three shapes, for the
  end of a video or a clip. It is not used on the site.
- **A repair.** A drawn line began as a dot: a round end is drawn on a dash of no
  length. Drawn lines now start a little further back than their own length.

## Not changed, on purpose

- No status, record, claim wording or card image changes.
- The favicon, launcher icons, link-preview cards and the embed stay still.
- Nothing starts on scroll, and no answer waits for an animation: the popup shows its
  answer the moment it is ready, whatever the capsule is doing.
- The study in which the capsule's blue half arrives after its coral half is not on
  any page: beside a status it could be read as bad then good. It appears only in
  the video sting, where no status is shown.
- The explainer video is not re-rendered.
- No extension version is changed; the Safari release in review is untouched.

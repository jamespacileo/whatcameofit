# Explainer video on the method page

## Why
A reader who arrives from a shared link has one page of prose to learn what the site is. A short video shows the same thing on one claim: the page, its records, how a status is set, the link lookup and the extension. The operator asked for it on 2026-11-13, for the method page, social posts and YouTube.

## Changes
- A `video/` Remotion project builds the explainer in 16:9 and 9:16 from a narration script. Every record it shows is copied from the published export, with the date the registries were checked.
- The method page shows the 16:9 video under its lede, as a file of this site. It never plays on its own, nothing of it is fetched before the reader presses play, its caption dates the records in it, and its words are on the page in a fold.
- No status, claim page, lookup or extension behaviour changes.

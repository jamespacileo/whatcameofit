# Candidate-first extension results

## Why
The working Safari lookup puts several paragraphs before the first candidate. Readers need to recognise the likely records quickly, especially in a small popup.

## Changes
Bring candidate titles and match confidence into the first screen. Use labelled icons, blue for match information and neutral trial metadata. Collapse provenance, confidence explanations, registry identifiers and privacy information without removing them. Keep automatic/unreviewed provenance, cache age, uncertainty and medical limitations visible. Preserve the explicit first-use sharing choice and all lookup behaviour.

# Visible content lookup and on-demand results

## Why
A Reddit post's claim exists inside its image while its DOM caption says only Dental revolution. The current reader ignores the image and reports no specific treatment. Readers reasonably expect the extension to understand the content they can see.

## Changes
Use automatic bounded screenshot fallback when the popup opens on an untracked media post, per operator instruction. Reuse private local results and shared independently public results on demand. Keep all automatic findings explicitly unreviewed. Preserve the $13 project allowance. Supersedes the older no-page-address and no-results-retention restrictions only for the cleaned context/cache address and bounded structured result caches described here.
